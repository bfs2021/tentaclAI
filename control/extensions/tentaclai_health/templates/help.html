The built-in module Service-Health runs svc test for every monitored service on a schedule (every 5 minutes by default) – the same test as the Test button of a service. It needs no approval and writes no audit entries. Disabled services and services with an empty ${VAR} are skipped.

  • Health (sidebar) – the state per service (up, partly: one part of the service fails, down), since when, a bar of the last 24 hours (30 minutes per block, the worst result counts), the availability over 24 hours and 7 days, and a summary per system. Check now runs a round at once.
  • The badge next to Health counts services that failed as many checks in a row as set for notifications.
  • Settings → Health – interval, after how many failed checks in a row a notification goes out (default 2) and which services are monitored.
  • Notifications: the events Service down (high – passes quiet hours) and Service up again. While the sandbox isn't running nothing is checked – the event Container stopped covers that.
  • The history (7 days) is kept in data/control/ext/tentaclai_health/; Export / Import carries the settings only.
